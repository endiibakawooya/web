This Privacy Policy explains how KIBIRAMABIRA handles information when you use Ikapuuni. Ikapuuni is designed as an offline memory game and does not require an account, login, or internet connection to play.

1Information we collect

Ikapuuni does not collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share personal information through the game. We do not ask for your name, phone number, location, contacts, photos, account credentials, or other personal details.

There are no user accounts, advertising SDKs, analytics services, or in-app tracking mechanisms described for this version of Ikapuuni.

2Local game data

Ikapuuni may save limited gameplay information locally on your device, such as your best score and game preferences, so the game can remember your progress. This information stays on your device and is not sent to KIBIRAMABIRA.

You can remove locally stored game data by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app, subject to the controls provided by your device.

3Permissions and device access

Ikapuuni is designed not to request access to sensitive device features or data. It does not require access to your location, camera, microphone, contacts, files, SMS, call logs, or similar personal information to play.

4Internet, advertising and third parties

The game is designed to work offline. This version does not require a network connection for gameplay and does not include third-party advertising, account sign-in, or third-party analytics described by this policy.

5Children and age suitability

Ikapuuni is intended for users aged 13 and above. Because the game does not require registration and is designed not to collect personal information, KIBIRAMABIRA does not knowingly collect personal information from children through Ikapuuni.

If you believe information has been provided to us outside the game by a child in a way that raises a privacy concern, please contact us using the details below.

6Security

Because gameplay data is kept locally and the game is designed not to transmit personal information, exposure of personal data through Ikapuuni is minimized. Users should still maintain appropriate security on their own devices.

7Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Ikapuuni's features or privacy practices change. If a future version introduces features that affect data handling, this policy should be updated accordingly. The effective date at the top of this page indicates when this policy was last revised.
